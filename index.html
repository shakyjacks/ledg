HOW THE TIES WERE BROKEN

LEDG tiebreak rules
  1. Two teams tied: head-to-head record, then points scored.
  2. Three or more tied: a team that swept every other tied team wins the tiebreak; a team swept by every other tied team finishes last of the group. With no sweeps, points scored decides.
  3. After a sweeper is placed, the teams still tied go through the same rules again (so if two remain, it's back to head-to-head).
